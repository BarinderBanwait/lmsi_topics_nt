This is the webpage for the one-day course in number theory at the Lodha Mathematical Sciences Institute in Mumbai.

## Tentative Schedule

| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 9:00 AM | Arrival |
| 9:30 AM | Informal address with students and parents |
| 9:45 AM | Session 1 — First half |
| 11:00 AM | Break |
| 11:20 AM | Session 1 — Second half |
| 12:50 PM | Lunch |
| 2:00 PM | Session 2 — Lecture + Problem session |
| 4:30 PM | Tea break |
| 5:00–5:45 PM | Q&A and panel discussion |

**Session 1** will be led by Dr. Banwait, and will cover: Pythagorean triples, congruent numbers, a basic introduction to elliptic curves, and the chord-and-tangent process.

**Session 2** will be led by Dr. O'Desky, and will cover solving polynomial equations modulo a prime.
